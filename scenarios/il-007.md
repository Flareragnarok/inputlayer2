## Context

("ideal for now"), its buttons are **quicks**, and the five named quicks
are prefixed with quick instead of suffixed with key: **quick layout**,
**quick suggestions**, **quick cursor**, **quick language**, **quick edit**
(owner, 2026-11-06: "instead of suffixing key, prefix them with quick"). Everywhere: Studio, toasts,
the Keys screen, docs, code. Build 80 had called the bar "quicksettings";
settings saved under either earlier name (the module in use, its height,
its place in the order of the parts) move to the new names on first
start, once.

1. Install over a build that had a quicksbar module, a changed quicksbar

2. Open the keyboard; open Studio and Studio → Keys and behaviours.

## Expected
- The quicksbar and its quicks look and sit exactly as before the update.
- Studio, the Keys screen and the toasts say quicksbar / quicks / quick
  layout… only.

build 80, old module files).
